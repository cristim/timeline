10 · Roadmap & Milestones

The biggest product risk is not whether PostgreSQL can store 100 million facts (it can); it is whether a person looking at the map and timeline can make sense of them. So the first milestone is a fun-test, not an ingestion system.

Phase 0: Interaction prototype (the go/no-go gate)

ROAD-1Prototype with 10k–50k hand-selected entities

Question to answer: is zooming through all of this actually fun and understandable?

  • Dataset: 10k–50k entities imported/curated across the full range: Big Bang, geology, dinosaurs, civilizations, major wars, science, inventions, cars, aircraft, books, games, current events, future projections. Includes the deep-time seed set (SRC-8).
  • Build only: timeline interaction (FE-2) + map synchronization (FE-3) + semantic zoom over static precomputed buckets (ZOOM-3/4) + minimal inspector (FE-6). Serving can be static JSON on a CDN; no accounts, no search backend, no tiles pipeline (borders may be a single canned layer).
  • Demo script = the golden views in ZOOM-5, executed with real hands on real hardware, including a tablet.

Exit criteria (all required to proceed):

  • A first-time user can travel Big Bang → WWII → today → far future without instruction and narrate what they are seeing.
  • Zoom/pan holds 60 fps with 2,000 on-screen objects on a mid-range laptop.
  • The golden views read correctly (right things appear/disappear at each level).
  • At least 3 of 5 outside testers keep exploring past 10 minutes unprompted (the fun test).

Before writing prototype code: spend a day inside Wikitime, ChronoAtlas, Chronos Atlas, OpenHistoricalMap, Histography, Histropedia with a written test checklist, and run the Wikidata census below (ROAD-2). If the prototype fails the fun test after honest iteration, stop or re-scope; do not proceed to ingestion.

ROAD-2Wikidata census (parallel to prototype)

Quantify the raw material before betting on it: process a Wikidata dump and report, per century and per type: how many battles, wars, political events, disasters, scientific events, people, species, products exist; how many have dates + coordinates + Wikipedia sitelinks; distribution of time precision. Output: a report that sets realistic MVP HOT-tier targets (SRC-5) and validates the importance signals (ZOOM-2).
