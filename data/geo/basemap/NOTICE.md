# Protomaps basemap provenance and licences

`protomaps-20260829-z0-6.pmtiles` is a global zoom 0-6 extract of
`https://build.protomaps.com/20260829.pmtiles`, made with
`github.com/protomaps/go-pmtiles@v1.30.0` under Go 1.26.7. The distributed
archive is an ODbL Produced Work.

- OpenStreetMap and `osmdata.openstreetmap.de` data under the Open Data Commons

  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and
  https://osmdata.openstreetmap.de/info/license.html. The licence text is at
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/.

  https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.
- Daylight landcover derived from ESA WorldCover under CC BY 4.0:
  https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/ and
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
